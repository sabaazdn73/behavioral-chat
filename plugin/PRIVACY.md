# Behavioral Chat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Behavioral Chat is a Claude plugin made of instructions and keyword lists. It has no server, no
code of its own, and no connection to the author.

## What is processed

Only the chat export that you upload to Claude yourself (a Telegram Desktop or WhatsApp export of a
chat you took part in). Claude reads it inside your own conversation to compute the analysis shown
there.

## What is stored, shared or sold

Nothing. The plugin does not store, log, transmit, sell or share any chat content or results. It
uses no accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising or third-party processors. The author never
receives your data. How Claude itself handles your conversations is governed by Anthropic's own
privacy policy.

## The other person in the chat

A chat export contains personal data of a second person. You are responsible for having a lawful
reason to process it, for only analyzing chats you took part in, and for not using the results to
monitor, pressure or harm anyone.

## Children

The plugin is not intended for people under 18.
